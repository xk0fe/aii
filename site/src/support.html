Support

Write to {{contact}} and we’ll get back to you. Say which computer you use (Mac or Windows) and, if it’s about the phone app, which phone. Never send passwords or API keys.

Getting started

{{product}} runs Claude Code or Codex on your computer. Install one of them and sign in to it once, then open {{product}} and add your first agent.

Installing

  • Mac: open the DMG and drag {{product}} to Applications. If your Mac says it can’t check the app, open System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.
  • Windows: run the installer. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  • Phone: get the app from the App Store or Google Play, then scan the pairing code {{product}} shows on your computer.

The phone doesn’t connect

Keep {{product}} open on your computer and put the phone on the same Wi-Fi (or the same Tailscale network). If it still can’t reach the computer, make a new pairing code on the computer and scan it again.

What it costs

{{product}} is free. Your agents run on your own Claude or Codex account, so their work counts toward that account’s plan or usage.

Removing {{product}}

On a Mac, drag {{product}} from Applications to the Trash. On Windows, open Settings › Apps and uninstall it. Your clique stays in the .clique folder in your home folder; delete that folder too if you want your clique gone. Claude Code and Codex keep their own history of the conversations in .claude and .codex.

Your data

{{product}} collects nothing about you. The privacy policy says exactly what goes where.

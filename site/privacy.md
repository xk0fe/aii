# Privacy policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Crew is made by Green Grass Inc. This policy covers the Crew apps for Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android, and this website.

## The short version

Crew collects nothing about you. There are no Crew accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We don't run servers that your work passes through. Your agents, chats and settings stay on your own computers and phones.

## What stays on your devices

- **Your crew.** Your agents, their instructions, chats, tasks, notes and settings are kept in a folder on your computer (`.crew` in your home folder). Delete the folder and they're gone.
- **Your phone's pairings.** The phone app keeps the computers it's paired with, and the keys for them, in the phone's secure storage (the iPhone's Keychain, Android's Keystore). The keys never leave the phone.
- **Your voice.** Dictation runs on your computer, and what you say isn't sent anywhere. On a Mac it uses Apple's on-device speech recognition; the first time you dictate in a new language, macOS may download Apple's speech model for it. On Windows the speech model comes inside Crew.

## What leaves your devices, and where it goes

- **To the AI you use.** Crew runs Claude Code and Codex on your computer, signed in with your own account. What you and your agents send them (your messages, the files they read, the output of commands they run, pictures you attach) goes to Anthropic for Claude or OpenAI for Codex, under your agreement with them and their privacy policies. Crew doesn't see your password and doesn't add anything of its own. If you set up another provider, such as GLM, that provider gets it instead; a local model (for example through Ollama) stays on your computer.
- **Between your own computers and phones.** When you link your computers or pair a phone, they talk to each other directly, over your own network (or your Tailscale network, if you use Tailscale). The connection is encrypted, and only devices you paired with a pairing code can join. Nothing goes through a server of ours.
- **Links you open** open in your web browser.
- **Spell checking on Windows.** The first time you type in a text box, the spell checker may download a word list for your language from Google's servers. It doesn't send what you type.

## What we collect

Nothing. Crew has no analytics, crash reporting, usage statistics, update checks or advertising IDs, and it doesn't sell or share data, because it has none to share.

## The App Store and Google Play

If you get the phone app from the App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google handles the download under their own privacy policies. If you've chosen on your phone to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple or Google may show us crash reports and download numbers that don't say who you are. We use them only to fix problems.

## This website and the downloads

The website and the Mac and Windows downloads are hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages and GitHub Releases). Like any web server, GitHub may record your IP address and browser when you visit or download, as its privacy statement describes. The website itself uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other sites.

## What the apps ask your permission for

- **Microphone and speech recognition** (Mac and Windows): only while you dictate a message.
- **Local network** (Mac and iPhone): to reach your other computers and your phone.
- **Camera** (phone): only to scan the pairing code your computer shows. The picture isn't kept or sent.
- **Notifications** (Mac and Windows): to tell you on your computer when an agent needs you.

## Children

Crew isn't meant for children under 13.
